# Chemical Engineering Laboratory Portfolio

This repository presents selected laboratory work completed as part of my B.Tech. in Chemical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar. The portfolio focuses on liquid-phase thermodynamics, mixing, heat transfer and fluid flow - areas relevant to process development, scale-up and beverage manufacturing.

Rather than serving as a collection of every course experiment, the repository highlights work that best demonstrates my ability to conduct experiments, maintain structured records, perform engineering calculations, interpret results and communicate technical findings.

## Featured Experiments

| Experiment | Engineering focus | Relevance to process and product R&D |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Heat Transfer in an Agitated Vessel | Mixing, transient heating and heat-transfer performance | Illustrates temperature control and mixing considerations in batch processing |
| Plate Heat Exchanger | Energy balance, overall heat-transfer coefficient and exchanger performance | Relevant to rapid heating and cooling operations used in liquid-product processing |
| Shell-and-Tube Heat Exchanger | Thermal duty, temperature profiles and equipment performance | Demonstrates process-scale heat-transfer analysis and interpretation |
| Vapor-Liquid Equilibrium | Phase equilibrium and liquid-vapor composition relationships | Builds understanding of mixture behavior and volatile-component processing |
| Infinite-Dilution Activity Coefficient | Non-ideal liquid behavior at very low solute concentration | Relevant to understanding interactions involving dilute components such as flavors and aromas |
| Turbulent-Flow Heat Transfer | Flow-regime effects, empirical correlations and Wilson-plot analysis | Demonstrates data-driven evaluation under industrially relevant flow conditions |

Additional experiments in vapor pressure, laminar-flow heat transfer, fluid measurement, pressure drop, pumps, thermal conductivity and related transport phenomena are summarized separately where relevant.

## What Each Experiment Contains

Depending on the experiment and the material available for public sharing, each folder includes:

- A concise overview of the objective and engineering principle
- Experimental setup or an independently prepared schematic
- Recorded observations and anonymized raw data
- Excel calculation sheets or calculation summaries
- Plots and performance comparisons
- Discussion of results, deviations and experimental limitations
- A structured technical report
- A short note connecting the underlying principle to process or product development

## Skills Demonstrated

- Bench-scale experimental work and safe laboratory practice
- Accurate observation and maintenance of laboratory records
- Formula-based calculations, mass and energy balances
- Microsoft Excel for calculations, tabulation and engineering plots
- Interpretation of trends, deviations and sources of experimental error
- Technical report writing and presentation of results

## Notes on Interpretation

These are supervised academic laboratory experiments rather than commercial product-development trials. Any connection to beverage or consumer-product R&D refers to the transferability of the engineering principles and laboratory skills. Results are reported for the specific apparatus, operating conditions and materials used during the experiments and should not be treated as production-scale design recommendations.

## Academic Integrity and Attribution

Only my own reports, calculations, plots and permitted experimental data are included. Laboratory manuals, classmates' reports, restricted course materials and personally identifiable teammate information are not reproduced. Apparatus schematics included in the repository are independently prepared unless another source is explicitly credited.
